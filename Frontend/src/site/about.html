02 — Our Journey

From a counter in Shiyabaug to a 2-hour network

2014

The first counter

Darsh Dental Depot opens at Shiyabaug with a simple rule — stock only what we would use in our own clinic: authentic, in-date and fairly priced.

2014

Vraj Vihar Complex

The depot moves to FF-10/11, Vraj Vihar Complex at Char Rasta, Kevdabaug — a proper showroom with dedicated storage for temperature-sensitive materials.

2018

Major brand partnerships

Authorised distribution for 3M, Ivoclar, GC, Dentsply Sirona, MANI and Septodont turns the counter into the city's dependable source for originals.

2022

2-hour express delivery

Our own rider network launches — orders placed before 7:30 PM reach clinics across every major Vadodara sector within two hours.

2026

The digital depot

This platform brings the counter online — 24/7 catalog access, WhatsApp ordering and automated GST invoicing for registered doctors.
